Privacy Policy

PouchLess is designed to keep the sensitive details of your quit journey on your device. We do not create an account, ask for your name or email address, sell personal information, or use advertising trackers.

Data stored only on your device

Your profile inputs, quit and taper dates, streak, savings inputs, slips, craving outcomes, notification choices, coach history, and AI consent choice are stored locally on your device. They remain there until you use “Clear local data,” delete the app, or the operating system removes app storage. PouchLess has no account backup and cannot recover this data.

When data leaves your device

Subscriptions

If you view, purchase, or restore a subscription, Apple processes payment and RevenueCat processes subscription status. They may receive purchase history, app and device identifiers, country, and diagnostic data needed to operate subscriptions. PouchLess does not receive your full payment-card details.

AI coach

The AI coach is optional. Before its first network use, PouchLess asks for consent to send recent coach messages and a limited summary of your pouch-use profile through a PouchLess Cloudflare proxy to OpenAI. The proxy rate-limits requests using short-lived, secret-salted hashes of network addresses; the app sends no persistent user or device identifier with coach requests. OpenAI response storage is disabled. If you decline or the service is unavailable, the app uses local, prewritten support. Coach content is never sent to analytics.

Product analytics

Production and preview builds use PostHog for a short allowlist of anonymous product events, such as onboarding step number, paywall view, purchase outcome, app-life-cycle day bucket, panic-tool use, and coach mode. Events use a random device identifier. Pouch profile fields, reasons, chat text, slips notes, and health details are explicitly excluded. Person profiles, session replay, touch autocapture, console capture, and advertising features are disabled.

Crash diagnostics

Production and preview builds use Sentry for app and device diagnostics, stack traces, app version, and crash-free-session measurement. Default personally identifying information is disabled. Request bodies, user identity, and breadcrumb payloads are removed before an event is sent. Do not put private information in operating system crash descriptions.

Notifications and sharing

Reminder scheduling occurs through the operating system on your device. Share cards leave the app only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet; that destination’s privacy policy then applies.

Children

PouchLess is not for people under 18 and does not knowingly collect their information. The app asks users to confirm they are at least 18 before entering pouch-use information.

Your choices

  • Use the free, local tools without the AI coach.
  • Decline notifications in the app or iOS Settings.
  • Clear local data from Settings at any time.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account settings.

Service providers and international processing

Apple, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, OpenAI, PostHog, and Sentry process data under their own terms and retention controls, potentially in the United States or other countries. We configure each service to minimize collection for the purposes described above.

Contact and changes

Use the support page for privacy requests. Because PouchLess has no accounts, include only the app version and a non-sensitive description. We may update this policy when features or providers change and will revise the effective date.
